# Birthday — playful but real
Another year of you, which means another year of me being slightly smug about knowing you before everyone else figured out how great you are. I hope today is exactly as ridiculous and good as you are. Eat the bigger slice. You've earned the whole cake, honestly.

---

# Thank you — specific and grounded
I don't think you realize you did anything. You just showed up with coffee and didn't ask me to explain myself, and somehow that was the exact thing. I've been carrying that morning around for weeks. Thank you for being the kind of person who knows when not to say much.

---

# Apology — honest, no excuses
I've started this a few times and kept deleting the part where I explain myself, because the explaining isn't the point. I was wrong, and I'd rather just say that plainly than dress it up. You deserved better from me, and I'm going to be better. I miss how easy things are with us.

---

# Long-distance / missing someone
It's the small ones that get me — going to text you something stupid and remembering you're three time zones away. Distance is mostly fine until it suddenly isn't. Just know that wherever I am, some part of the day is always quietly reserved for thinking about you.

---

# Encouragement before something hard
You don't have to feel ready. Nobody who's ever done something brave felt ready first — they just went, shaking a little, and figured it out on the way. I've seen you do harder things than this without even noticing they were hard. Go be exactly as much as you already are.
